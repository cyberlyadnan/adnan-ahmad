## Overview

**LitVerse** is a full-stack web app for discovering, reviewing, and discussing books. It combines AI-powered summaries and recommendations with community reviews, favorites, and a conversational assistant.

---

## Key Features

- 🤖 **AI Assistance** – Gemini-powered summaries, review-title suggestions, and context-aware chat.
- 🔍 **Discovery Tools** – Advanced search, genre and rating filters, featured and similar books.
- ⭐ **Community Reviews** – Rich text reviews, ratings, favorites, and user profiles with stats.
- 🌓 **Delightful UI** – Tailwind + shadcn components, animations, dark mode, and optimized images.
- 🔐 **Secure Auth** – Email/Google sign-in, protected routes, and Firestore-backed sessions.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js 13 (App Router)**, **React 18**, **TypeScript**
- **Tailwind CSS**, **shadcn/ui**, **Framer Motion**
- **Firebase**: Firestore, Auth, Storage
- **Gemini AI / OpenAI** integration for summaries and chat

---

## Outcome

Launched an AI-augmented reading platform that scales to millions of records with real-time sync and a polished, community-first experience.
