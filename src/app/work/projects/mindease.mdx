## Overview

**MindEase** is a complete **mental wellness application** built to help users reduce stress, track emotions, and practice guided relaxation.  
The app integrates an AI chatbot for supportive conversations and personalized emotional guidance — making it a unique blend of wellness + technology.

This project demonstrates skills in **mobile app development**, **AI integration**, and **full backend architecture.**

---

## Key Features

- 💬 **AI Chatbot Support**  
  Powered by OpenAI GPT for stress relief, journaling prompts & emotional guidance.

- 😊 **Mood Tracking**  
  Users record daily emotions and view long-term trends.

- 📝 **Personal Journaling**  
  Secure space for writing daily reflections.

- 🌬️ **Breathing & Relaxation Tools**  
  Guided breathing animation + calming sound sessions.

- 🔐 **JWT Authentication**  
  Secure email-based login system.

- 🧪 **Onboarding Personality Quiz**  
  Personalizes recommendations and mood experience.
