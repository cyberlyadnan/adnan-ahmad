## Overview

**DairyPro** is a digital operations app built to modernize daily milk management for dairy owners and field workers.  
It replaces manual paper-based processes with a clean, automated, mobile-first workflow that works even in rural offline environments.

Designed for speed, accuracy, and simplicity — making daily operations effortless and transparent.

---

## Key Features

- 🥛 **Daily Milk Entry System**  
  Track milk quantity, fat %, SNF, and rates with automated calculations.

- 👥 **Customer Management**  
  Maintain records of customers, suppliers, and their transactions.

- 📊 **Reports & Ledgers**  
  Generate daily/weekly/monthly milk and payment reports instantly.

- 📡 **Offline-Friendly Workflow**  
  Designed for field workers in rural areas with intermittent connectivity.

- 💰 **Rate Charts & Pricing**  
  Automated pricing based on fat/SNF values.

- 🔐 **Secure Login**  
  Role-based access for owners and field workers.
