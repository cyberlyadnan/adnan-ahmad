## Overview

**Arabic Learning Institute Platform** pairs a marketing site with a full-featured student portal. Students register, manage profiles, access resources, and submit monthly fees with real-time status updates, while the public site highlights courses, testimonials, and Islamic-calendar-aligned programming.

---

## Key Features

- 🧑‍🎓 **Student Portal** – Authentication, protected dashboard, profile editing, status badges, and student IDs.
- 📂 **Learning Resources** – Organized materials, duas, assessments, and tests with searchable tables and file metadata.
- 💳 **Fee Management** – QR/UPI payments, transaction tracking, monthly detection, and approval workflow.
- 🎨 **Modern UX** – Responsive design, dark mode, smooth loading states, and toast-driven feedback.
- 🔒 **Secure Access** – Route guards, schema validation, and Firebase-backed auth/session handling.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js 13 (App Router)**, **React 18**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**
- **Firebase**: Authentication, Firestore, Storage
- **Radix UI**, **Framer Motion**, **React Hook Form + Zod**

---

## Outcome

Delivered an enterprise-ready education platform that scales to thousands of learners with real-time data sync, polished UX, and production-grade authentication.
