## Overview

**Volta Line** is a Dubai-based electrical and technical services company that needed a **professional and trustworthy digital identity** to attract B2B clients across the GCC region.  
The goal was to build a clean, modern, SEO-ready website that clearly communicates services and drives service inquiries.

This project showcases experience in **corporate website development**, **UI/UX for B2B**, and **SEO-focused web architecture**.

---

## Key Features

- 🏢 **Corporate Website Design**  
  Modern, minimal, Dubai-standard UI for professional services.

- 📁 **Service Catalog**  
  Structured sections for electrical, maintenance & technical offerings.

- 📞 **Lead Generation System**  
  Inquiry CTAs placed strategically for maximum conversions.

- 🌍 **SEO Optimization**  
  Built with semantic structure & on-page SEO for GCC search ranking.

- 📱 **Fully Responsive**  
  Tailored layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

The website helped Volta Line present a credible, premium brand identity in the UAE market.  
It now acts as a **lead-generation engine**, increasing service inquiries and strengthening their business presence in the region.
