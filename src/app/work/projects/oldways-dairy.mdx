## Overview

**Oldway’s Dairy Management System** digitizes end-to-end dairy operations across three roles: Admin, Field Worker, and Customer. It covers animal health, milk collection/delivery, payments, complaints, inventory, and business analytics with bilingual UX.

---

## Key Features

- 🐄 **Animal & Milk Operations** – Milk collection/delivery, quality tracking, pricing, and inventory.
- 📊 **Analytics Dashboards** – Real-time KPIs, revenue/expense tracking, and downloadable reports.
- 👥 **Role-Based Panels** – Admin controls, field workflows, and customer self-service with payments.
- 🌐 **Bilingual & Offline** – English/Hindi support, transliteration, caching, and offline-ready flows.
- 🔔 **Automated Notifications** – Reminders for vaccinations, payments, complaints, and delivery status.

---

## Tech Stack

- **React Native (Expo)**, **TypeScript**, **Redux/Context**
- **Firebase**: Firestore, Auth (Email/Phone OTP), Cloud Functions, Storage, FCM
- **Google Cloud Translation API**, **Expo services** for device capabilities

---

## Outcome

Delivered a field-ready mobile suite that streamlines dairy logistics, improves transparency for customers, and provides admins with actionable intelligence.
