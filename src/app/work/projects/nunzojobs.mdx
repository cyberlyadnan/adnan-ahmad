## Overview

**NunzoJobs** delivers instant bookings for coolies and 18+ worker categories with real-time availability, location-aware pricing, OTP verification, and dual payment gateways for scale.

---

## Key Features

- ⚡ **Instant Booking** – Coolie and worker flows with live pricing, status tracking, and booking history.
- 💳 **Dual Payments** – Razorpay + Stripe, online/cash handling, payouts, and receipt generation.
- 🌍 **Geo Services** – Google Places search, location history, platform-based pricing, and GeoFirestore.
- 🧭 **Role-Specific Dashboards** – Admin, provider, and user panels with approvals, earnings, and analytics.
- 🔒 **Secure Access** – Firebase Auth, role-based routing, OTP verification, and protected APIs.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js 16 (App Router)**, **React 19**, **TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**
- **Redux Toolkit + Zustand**, **Firebase** (Auth, Firestore, Storage)
- **Razorpay & Stripe**, **Leaflet/Google Places**, **GeoFirestore**

---

## Outcome

Built a production-ready marketplace handling multi-role workflows, real-time updates, and scalable payment operations across web and mobile form factors.
